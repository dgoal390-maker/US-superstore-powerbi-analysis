# US-superstore-powerbi-analysis
US Superstore Sales and Profitability Analysis using Power BI

**Project Overview**
This project analyses US Superstore Sales, profitability, order volume, discount patterns, and product-level performance using power BI.

The objective is to identify business trends, highlight loss-making products, and provide actionable recommendations to improve profitability.

**Business Objectives**
* Analyse sales and profit across product categories.
* Compare profit margins and average order value(AOV).
* investigate relationship between discounts and profitability.
* Identify the most profitable and loss-making products.
* Recommend actions based on the analysis.

**Tools used**
* Microsoft power BI.
* Power Query.
* DAX
* Data visualisation and business Analysis.

**Report Pages**
1.Executive Overview-- Sales, Profit, Profit margin, orders, and AOV.
2.Profitability and Discount Analysis-- Discount patterns and Profitability metrics.
3.Product Profitability and Diagnostics -- Top-performing and loss-making products.
4. Business Investigation -- Product-level cost and margin daignostics.

**key Business Insights**

**1.office Supplies Performance**
Office Supplies recorded 871 orders approximately $89,525 in profit, and a profit margin of 16.24%. Its average order value was approximately $633.

**Business implication**: Investigate opportunities to increase average order value while preserving the categorys profitability.

**2. Furniture Profitability**
Furniture had the highest average order value, approximately $1,805, but the lowest category profit margin at 8.97%.

**Business implication**: Review product-level margins, discounting, and cost drivers rather than assuming that higher order values guarantee stronger profitability.

**3.Loss-Making Products**
The Polycom Viewstation ISDN Videoconferencing Unit recorded approximately $46,539 in Sales and loss of $27,621, with a profit margin of approximately -59.35%.

**Business implication**: Investigate pricing, discounts, costs, and transaction details for this product before deciding whether to change its pricing or sales strategy.

**4. Discount and profitability Patterns**
Higher discount bands were associated with lower profit margins for Furniture and Office Supplies in this dataset.Technology showed a different pattern.

**Business implication**: Review discount policies by category and product . This analysis identifies an association, not proof that discounts caused the lower margins.

**5.Product Cost Diagnostics**
Some products showed implied costs per unit higher than sales per unit.

**Business implication**: Validate product costs, discount rules, pricing, and source data before making operational decisions.

**Recommendations**
*Investigate products with consistently negative profit.
*Review category-specific discount policies.
*Monitor profit margin alongside sales and order volume.
*Validate product-level cost and pricing data.
*Track changes in profitability after any business action.

**Analytical Limitation**
The Cost measure in this report is calculated as:
  cost = Sales - Profit

Therefore, cost and implies cost per unit are derived metrics, not independently verified supplier or purchase cost. Business recommendations should be validated against the original accounting data.
